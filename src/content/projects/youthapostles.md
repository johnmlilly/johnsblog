## Problem

- **Outdated design.** The site looked dated next to the community it represents.
- **Unclear calls to action.** Visitors couldn't easily tell how to learn more, connect, or support the mission.

## Approach

- Refreshed the visual design in Squarespace.
- Reorganized navigation and content around what visitors come for.
- Gave each page a clear next step.
