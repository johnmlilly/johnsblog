## Problem

A family member's new podcast needed a site that put episodes front and center, loaded quickly, and didn't need a server to babysit.

## Approach

- **Fast static site.** Built with 11ty and hosted on Netlify: plain HTML, quick to load, and cheap to run.
- **Episode layout.** Episodes are laid out so listeners can find the latest one and browse past ones.
- **Easy updates.** Decap CMS gives a simple editor for adding content without touching code.
