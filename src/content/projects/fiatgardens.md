## Problem

A growing local flower farm needed a home online that matched the care they put into their flowers.

## Approach

- Designed, built, and launched a static Webflow site end to end.
- Focused on telling their story, so visitors get a feel for the farm.
- Set it up so the owners can make updates themselves in Webflow.
