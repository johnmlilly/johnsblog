## Problem

Parishioners had trouble finding everyday information on the old site, and updating it was harder than it needed to be for staff.

## Approach

- Redesigned the site around what parishioners look for most.
- Rebuilt it in WordPress with Elementor so staff can edit pages visually.
- Added custom CSS for the details the page builder couldn't handle on its own.
- Made every page work well on phones.
